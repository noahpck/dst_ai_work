#PLAYBOOK 2: Network Port Scanning & Reconnaissance
Framework: NIST SP 800-61 Rev. 2

##DETECTION SIGNALS:
- Single external IP hitting dozens of sequential ports (e.g. 22, 80, 443, 8080, 3389)
- Scanning occurring within a few seconds across your network space
- Firewall logs showing repeated connection attempts across multiple ports
- Alerts from pfSense, ufw, or Zeek/Bro connection logs

##ANALYSIS:
- Identify if it is a horizontal scan (one port, many hosts) or vertical scan (one host, many ports)
- Extract the scanning IP and timestamp range
- Determine which ports were probed and which responded
- Assess whether any open ports were successfully connected to

##CONTAINMENT:
- Generate a temporary perimeter block for the scanning IP
- iptables -A INPUT -s <IP> -j DROP
- Alert network team to monitor for follow-up exploitation attempts
- If scan was successful on any port, treat as potential intrusion

##ERADICATION/RECOVERY:
- Verify all listening services on discovered open ports are fully patched
- Hide sensitive services behind a VPN or firewall ACL
- Close any unnecessary open ports
- Review and tighten firewall ruleset

##POST-INCIDENT:
- Document which ports are exposed and why
- Consider deploying a honeypot to detect future reconnaissance
- Enable port scan detection rules in IDS/IPS
