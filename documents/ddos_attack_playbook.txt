# PLAYBOOK 5: Distributed Denial-of-Service (DDoS) Attack
Framework: NIST SP 800-61 Rev. 2

## DETECTION SIGNALS:
- Sudden spike in inbound network traffic from multiple source IP addresses
- Unusually high CPU, memory, or bandwidth utilization on public-facing servers
- Legitimate users report slow response times or inability to access services
- Firewall or Intrusion Detection System (IDS) generates alerts for excessive connection attempts
- Large number of incomplete TCP handshakes (SYN flood) or repeated requests to the same resource
- Monitoring tools detect abnormal traffic patterns or sustained traffic volumes above baseline

## ANALYSIS:
- Identify the targeted systems, applications, or services
- Determine the type of DDoS attack (e.g., SYN flood, UDP flood, HTTP flood, DNS amplification)
- Analyze traffic sources, protocols, ports, and request patterns
- Determine whether the attack is volumetric, protocol-based, or application-layer
- Assess the impact on service availability and business operations
- Identify whether the attack is being used to distract from other malicious activity

## CONTAINMENT:
- Activate DDoS mitigation services or upstream traffic filtering
- Apply firewall and router rules to block malicious IP addresses where appropriate
- Enable rate limiting on affected services
- Redirect traffic through a Content Delivery Network (CDN) or traffic scrubbing service
- Isolate non-critical services if necessary to preserve essential business operations
- Continuously monitor traffic to evaluate the effectiveness of mitigation measures

## ERADICATION:
- Remove temporary firewall or routing rules that are no longer required
- Verify that all malicious traffic has ceased
- Investigate whether any systems were compromised during the attack
- Patch and harden exposed services to reduce future attack surfaces
- Update intrusion detection and prevention signatures based on observed attack characteristics

## POST-INCIDENT:
- Review network traffic logs and DDoS mitigation effectiveness
- Document attack duration, scale, affected services, and response actions
- Update DDoS response procedures based on lessons learned
- Test network resilience and incident response plans through future exercises
- Review capacity planning and consider additional redundancy or mitigation services to improve resilience against future attacks
